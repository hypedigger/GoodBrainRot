# Asset credits — Wikimedia images

The images bundled in `app/src/main/assets/animals`, `dinos`, `vehicles` and `babies`
were downloaded by `scripts/fetch_content.ps1` from **Wikimedia Commons**: each one is the
500 px thumbnail of the lead image of the corresponding English Wikipedia article.
They are used under their individual licenses (mostly Creative Commons BY / BY-SA or public
domain); author and license for each file are listed below, as reported by the Wikimedia
Commons API. Click the source link for the full file description page.

Attribution resolved from the current article lead images on 2026-10-07. If an article image
was replaced on Wikipedia after the asset was downloaded, the credited file may differ from
the bundled one; such cases, when detected, are listed under "Unresolved".
